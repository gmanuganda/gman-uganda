About GMaN

GMaN - God's Manifestation Among Nations is a Social Work Organisation based in Kampala, Uganda. Formerly Equitas, we have transformed to a broader mission to manifest God's love through social justice, community empowerment, and child protection.

Our Services

  • Social Work Services - Family support, counselling and community development.
  • Justice Services - Legal aid and child rights advocacy.
  • Community Services - Education, health outreach and empowerment programmes.

🌍 Project BOYCHILD Global Initiative

Raising, protecting and empowering the Boy Child for a balanced future through mentorship, education and moral formation.
