Who we are

Professional social work with people at the centre.

Equitas Social Work & Justice Consultants is a Uganda-based professional consultancy. We work with organizations and communities facing social vulnerability, protection concerns, inequality and exclusion.

Child protection and safeguarding are our flagship specializations. We bring together social-work practice, community development, research, programme evaluation and organizational support to help clients move from understanding a challenge to taking practical action.

Equitas flagship programme

Project BOYCHILD

PROTECT · DEVELOP · PREPARE

A child-protection and positive-development programme supporting boys and adolescent males to grow into responsible, resilient, respectful and socially responsible men.

The programme combines structured learning with support from families, schools, mentors and communities—helping boys build skills, confidence and positive identities.

Life skillsMentorshipPositive masculinityFather engagementSchool engagementLeadershipChild protection
Partner on BOYCHILD →

Protect the Boy. Develop the Boy. Prepare the Man.

BOYCHILD recognizes boys as children with rights, strengths and developmental needs. Its approach builds support around each boy, not just within a classroom session.

  • Structured education and life-skills sessions
  • Mentorship and positive adult role models
  • Father, caregiver and school engagement
  • Leadership and community participation
