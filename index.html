About GMaN

GMaN - God's Manifestation Among Nations is a Social Work Organisation based in Kampala, Uganda. GMaN has a broader mission to manifest God's love through social justice, community empowerment, safeguarding and child protection.

We work with communities, families, organisations and institutions to promote dignity, safety, justice and social wellbeing.

Our Services

  • Social Work Services - Family support, counselling and community development.
  • Justice Services - Legal aid, child rights advocacy and justice support.
  • Community Services - Education, health outreach and empowerment programmes.
  • Child Protection - Safeguarding, child protection awareness and support.

🌍 Project BOYCHILD Global Initiative

Raising, protecting and empowering the Boy Child for a balanced future through mentorship, education and moral formation.

The initiative promotes positive development, education, responsible citizenship and protection of boys and young people.
